Product Overview
DEUPAN-DSR is a prescription-only combination capsule formulated to treat gastrointestinal disorders related to excess stomach acid production and impaired gut motility. It combines a fast-acting proton pump inhibitor (PPI) with a sustained-release prokinetic agent to offer long-lasting relief from acid reflux, heartburn, and associated nausea.
Key Benefits & Uses
Gastroesophageal Reflux Disease (GERD): Controls chronic acid backflow that causes intense heartburn and throat irritation.
Peptic and Duodenal Ulcers: Reduces aggressive stomach acid to promote the healing of open sores in the digestive tract lining.
Anti-Emetic Action: Suppresses feelings of nausea, vomiting, upper abdominal fullness, bloating, and gas.
Advanced Pellet Release: Uses enteric-coated and sustained-release technology to maximize continuous, 24-hour symptom management.
Composition & Active Ingredients
Each hard gelatin capsule contains:
Pantoprazole Sodium IP equivalent to Pantoprazole: 40 mg (As enteric coated pellets) — Blocks the specific proton pump enzymes responsible for releasing harsh stomach acid.
Domperidone IP: 30 mg (As sustained release pellets) — Increases upper digestive tract motility to help food move smoothly out of the stomach.
Excipients: q.s. (Quantity Sufficient)
Colour: Sunset Yellow & Brilliant Blue used in empty capsule shells & Pellets.
Safety Warnings & Precautions
Administration Guideline: Capsule to be swallowed as a whole & not to be opened, chewed or crushed.
Schedule H Drug: Prescription required. This medication should not be sold by retail without a valid medical practitioner's prescription.
Medical Conditions: Extra caution is advised for individuals with preexisting cardiac conditions (such as history of QT prolongation), low magnesium levels, or underlying liver and kidney diseases.
Lifestyle Advisory: Patients should limit or avoid alcohol, caffeine, smoking, and highly fried or spicy foods to optimize therapy and prevent stomach lining aggravation.
Storage & Handling
Store in a cool, dry & dark place.
Keep all medicine out of reach of children.
