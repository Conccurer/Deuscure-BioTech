Product Overview
RUXLIV Syrup is a prescription-only combination medication formulated to stimulate appetite, manage nutritional deficits, and support healthy liver functioning. By combining an effective appetite stimulant with a lipotropic bile-binding agent, this syrup addresses poor weight gain, loss of appetite, and underlying digestive sluggishness. The sugar-free formulation makes it widely suitable for broad patient demographics, including those keeping track of carbohydrate consumption.
Key Benefits & Uses
Appetite Stimulation: Effectively revives and boosts hunger cues to manage conditions like anorexia nervosa, poor dietary intake, or unexpected weight loss.
Enhances Liver Function: Aids hepatic metabolic pathways and helps protect liver tissues against fatty changes or toxic accumulation.
Accelerates Weight Gain: Helps pediatric and adult populations recovering from long illnesses optimize their nutritional assimilation and muscle mass development.
Composition & Active Ingredients
Each 5 ml of RUXLIV Syrup typically contains:
Cyproheptadine Hydrochloride IP: 2 mg (Appetite Stimulant) — Minimizes specific serotonin actions in the hypothalamus to activate hunger and naturally enhance nutritional cravings.
Tricholine Citrate: 275 mg (Lipotropic / Bile Acid Binding Agent) — Facilitates cholesterol clearance, removes harmful bile salts, and reduces systemic lipid stress on liver tissues.
Base: Flavored sugar-free syrupy base.
Safety Warnings & Precautions
Drowsiness Alert: A common side effect is temporary sleepiness or lethargy upon initial dosing. Advise patients not to drive or operate mechanical gear immediately after use.
Medical History Warning: Exercise strict professional caution if the user has a prior medical history of glaucoma, urinary retention, asthma, or benign prostatic hyperplasia.
Concurrent Substances: Advise against mixing this syrup with alcohol, tranquilizers, or sedatives as it can multiply central nervous system depression.
Storage & Handling
Store in a cool, dark, and dry place, ideally at a temperature below 25°C.
Protect completely from exposure to direct sunlight and moisture.
Shake well before use to ensure uniform drug distribution.
Keep the container out of reach and sight of children.
