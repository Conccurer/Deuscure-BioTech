Product Overview
DULEM-LC is a prescription-only combination anti-allergic and respiratory medication formulated to treat chronic allergic rhinitis and manage asthma symptoms. By combining a leukotriene receptor antagonist with a non-sedating antihistamine, it provides comprehensive, dual-action control over persistent sneezing, running nose, and respiratory inflammation.
Key Benefits & Uses
Allergic Rhinitis Relief: Alleviates chronic seasonal and perennial allergic symptoms, including runny nose, watery eyes, sneezing, and nasal itchiness.
Asthma Management: Helps control underlying airway inflammation and prevents sudden exercise-induced bronchospasms.
Reduces Respiratory Discomfort: Blocks the natural chemical messengers responsible for causing swelling and narrowing in the nasal passages and lungs.
Composition & Active Ingredients
Each uncoated tablet contains:
Montelukast Sodium IP equivalent to Montelukast: 10 mg (Leukotriene Receptor Antagonist) — Minimizes swelling and relaxes airway smooth muscles to make breathing easier.
Levocetirizine Hydrochloride IP: 5 mg (Second-Generation Antihistamine) — Selectively blocks histamine pathways to rapidly halt severe allergic reactions.
Excipients: q.s. (Quantity Sufficient)
Safety Warnings & Precautions
Schedule H Drug: Prescription required. This medication should not be sold by retail without a valid medical prescription from a Registered Medical Practitioner.
Drowsiness Alert: While levocetirizine is a non-sedating antihistamine, some individuals may still experience mild sleepiness or fatigue. Advise caution when driving or using heavy machinery.
Mental Health Advisory: Montelukast has been rarely linked to neuropsychiatric changes (such as vivid dreams, anxiety, or mood shifts) in select populations; consult a doctor if any changes occur.
Alcohol Interaction: Advise patients to avoid concurrent alcohol use as it can amplify potential drowsiness and decrease coordination.
Storage & Handling
Store in a cool & dry place below 25°C temperature.
Protect carefully from direct light & moisture.
Keep the medicine out of reach of children.
