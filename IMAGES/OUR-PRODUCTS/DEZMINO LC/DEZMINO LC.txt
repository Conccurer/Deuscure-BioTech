Product Overview
DEZMINO LC Tablets (subtitled "The Vitalizing Regenerator") is a high-potency, FSSAI-approved therapeutic nutraceutical supplement formulated to combat muscle weakness, nerve exhaustion, and tissue breakdown. By combining essential amino acids, tissue-repairing carnitine, and vital neurological cofactors, it systematically accelerates cellular energy production and supports overall metabolic vitality.
Key Benefits & Uses
Cellular Energy & Vitality: Combines L-Carnitine and essential amino acids to power muscle tissues, combat chronic physical fatigue, and reduce muscle cramps.
Neurological & Nerve Support: Delivers a high therapeutic dose of Cyanocobalamin (Mecobalamin) to maintain healthy nerve cells, repair myelin sheaths, and manage peripheral neuropathy.
Complete Amino Acid Replenishment: Provides an extensive matrix of essential branched-chain amino acids (BCAAs) to promote protein synthesis, tissue repair, and lean muscle preservation.
Red Blood Cell Production: Formulated with Folic Acid and Vitamin B12 to support healthy hemoglobin synthesis and red blood cell maturation.

Safety Warnings & Precautions
Category Classification: This product is a Proprietary Dietary Nutritional Food Supplement. It is strictly "NOT FOR MEDICINAL USE" and not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any chronic disease.
Usage Advisory: To be used as directed by the Dietician. Do not exceed the explicitly stated recommended daily consumption boundaries.
Phenylketonuria Warning: Contains L-Phenylalanine; individuals with underlying phenylketonuria or related metabolic amino acid disorders must seek specialist approval before consumption.
Storage Degradation Note: Appropriate overages of vitamins and amino acids are added to compensate for natural loss during storage. Improper storage can reduce active concentration.

Protect thoroughly from direct sunlight, light exposure, and ambient moisture.
