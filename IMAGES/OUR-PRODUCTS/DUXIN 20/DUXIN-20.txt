Product Overview
DUXIN-20 is a prescription-only Serotonin-Norepinephrine Reuptake Inhibitor (SNRI) medication formulated in a gastro-resistant tablet base. It contains Duloxetine, a neuropsychiatric agent that balances key chemical messengers in the brain and nervous system to effectively manage major depressive disorders, generalized anxiety, and persistent chronic somatic pain syndromes.
Key Benefits & Uses
Major Depressive Disorder (MDD): Helps regulate mood, interest, and daily emotional energy parameters by restoring optimal brain chemical balance.
Generalized Anxiety Disorder (GAD): Manages persistent, excessive cognitive worry and somatic tension symptoms.
Chronic Neuropathic & Musculoskeletal Pain: Effectively mitigates diabetic peripheral neuropathy, fibromyalgia pain, and chronic lower back or osteoarthritis discomfort.
Composition & Active Ingredients
Each gastro-resistant tablet contains:
Duloxetine Hydrochloride IP equivalent to Duloxetine: 20 mg (Serotonin-Norepinephrine Reuptake Inhibitor) — Prevents the reabsorption of serotonin and norepinephrine to enhance mood and suppress baseline pain signals.
Excipients: q.s. (Quantity Sufficient)
Colour: Brilliant Blue
Safety Warnings & Precautions
Administration Guideline: Tablet should be swallowed whole; it must not be cut, chewed, or crushed. The gastro-resistant coating prevents the active chemical from dissolving prematurely in stomach acid.
Schedule H Drug: Prescription required. This medication must not be sold by retail without a valid medical prescription from a Registered Medical Practitioner.
Abrupt Discontinuation Alert: Advise users never to stop therapy suddenly. Halting intake abruptly can cause severe withdrawal manifestations (discontinuation syndrome), including dizziness, headaches, paresthesia, or irritability.
Black Box Monitoring: Patients starting therapy should be monitored closely for unexpected changes in baseline behavioral patterns, mood fluctuations, or worsening anxiety.
Storage & Handling
Store in a cool, dry, and dark place below 25°C.
Protect thoroughly from direct light and structural moisture.
Keep the medicine safely out of reach of children.
