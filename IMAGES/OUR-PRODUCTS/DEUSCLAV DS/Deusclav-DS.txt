Product Overview
Deusclav-DS is a high-strength, prescription-only antibiotic powder for oral suspension formulated specifically for pediatric use. It pairs a penicillin-class antibiotic with a beta-lactamase inhibitor to combat bacterial resistance, making it highly effective against a wide variety of stubborn bacterial infections in children.
Key Benefits & Uses
Middle Ear Infections (Otitis Media): Effectively treats painful bacterial ear infections resistant to standard antibiotics.
Respiratory Tract Infections: Resolves acute bacterial sinusitis, tonsillitis, pharyngitis, and lower respiratory tract issues like bronchitis.
Skin & Soft Tissue Infections: Treats pediatric skin conditions, infected wounds, and localized abscesses caused by susceptible bacteria.
Urinary Tract Infections (UTIs): Targets and clears bacterial infections within the urinary bladder and passages.
Composition & Active Ingredients
Dry powder for reconstitution (6.6gm / 30ml). Once reconstituted, the active strengths typically align with a double-strength pediatric formula containing:
Amoxycillin Trihydrate IP (Penicillin Antibiotic) — Destroys invading bacteria by stopping them from constructing their protective cell walls.
Potassium Clavulanate IP (Beta-Lactamase Inhibitor) — Inactivates the defense enzymes produced by resistant bacteria, safeguarding the amoxicillin.
Directions for Reconstitution & Preparation
Shake the bottle well to loosen the dry powder.
Twist and open the vial of sterile water provided with the pack.
Slowly add the sterile water into the bottle up to the ring mark on the bottle.
Put the cap back on and shake the bottle vigorously.
Adjust the volume up to the ring mark by adding more sterile water if necessary, and shake again.
Safety Warnings & Precautions
For Paediatric Use Only: Formulated specifically for infants and children under strict clinical supervision.
Schedule H Prescription Drug: Warning: Not to be sold by retail without the prescription of a Registered Medical Practitioner.
Finish the Complete Course: Ensure the child finishes the entire prescribed course to avoid the development of drug-resistant bacteria.
Allergy Warning: Contraindicated in children with a known history of severe allergic reactions (anaphylaxis) to penicillin or cephalosporin antibiotics.
Storage & Handling
Prior to Reconstitution: Store the dry powder in a cool, dry, and dark place below 25°C.
After Reconstitution: Store the liquid suspension in a refrigerator. The reconstituted contents must be consumed within 7 days. Throw away any extra portion left over after 7 days.
Keep securely out of reach of children.
