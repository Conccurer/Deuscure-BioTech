Product Overview
Z-Rol Sachet (Prebiotic & Probiotic Sachets) is a multi-strain synbiotic health supplement available in a pleasant Mango & Pineapple Flavour. Formulated with a potent blend of beneficial probiotic bacterial strains, beneficial yeast, prebiotics, and essential Zinc, it helps restore and balance gut microflora, support smooth digestion, and boost immunity.

Key Benefits & Uses
- Restores Gut Microflora: Delivers a high concentration of beneficial bacteria (Lactobacillus and Bifidobacterium strains) and yeast (Saccharomyces boulardii) to restore natural gut balance, especially after antibiotic usage or stomach upset.
- Enhances Digestive Health: Fructooligosaccharides (FOS) act as prebiotics to nourish healthy gut bacteria, promoting improved digestion and nutrient absorption.
- Relieves Diarrhea & Indigestion: Helps reduce the frequency and severity of acute diarrhea, gastroenteritis, and irritable bowel symptoms.
- Strengthens Immune Response: Enriched with Zinc to reinforce mucosal immunity and support overall gut barrier integrity.
