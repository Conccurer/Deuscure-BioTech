Product Overview
Deusclav LB 625 is a prescription-only, broad-spectrum combination antibiotic tablet formulated to treat a wide variety of bacterial infections. It pairs a penicillin-class antibiotic with a beta-lactamase inhibitor to overcome bacterial resistance, while incorporating beneficial probiotic spores to safeguard natural intestinal flora and reduce antibiotic-associated digestive issues.
Key Benefits & Uses
Respiratory Tract Infections: Treats bacterial infections of the airway, including acute sinusitis, bronchitis, pneumonia, and tonsillitis.
Ear, Nose, and Throat Infections: Effectively resolves severe middle ear infections (otitis media) and throat complications.
Skin and Soft Tissue Infections: Manages deep bacterial skin infections, cellulitis, and wound infections.
Urinary Tract Infections (UTIs): Resolves complicated and uncomplicated bacterial bladder or kidney infections.
Gastrointestinal Protection: The integrated probiotic strain supports gut health, helping prevent diarrhea commonly caused by strong antibiotics.
Composition & Active Ingredients
Each film-coated tablet contains:
Amoxycillin Trihydrate IP equivalent to anhydrous Amoxycillin: 500 mg (Penicillin Antibiotic) — Destroys bacteria by weakening and disrupting their cell walls.
Potassium Clavulanate (Diluted) IP equivalent to Clavulanic Acid: 125 mg (Beta-Lactamase Inhibitor) — Prevents bacteria from destroying amoxicillin, broadening its effectiveness.
Lactic Acid Bacillus: 60 million spores (Probiotic) — Restores healthy gut microflora during intensive antibiotic therapy.
Excipients: q.s. (Quantity Sufficient)
Colour: Titanium Dioxide I.P.
Safety Warnings & Precautions
Complete the Full Course: Patients must finish the entire prescribed duration of therapy, even if they feel better, to stop the development of drug-resistant bacteria.
Schedule H Drug: Warning: Not to be sold by retail without the prescription of a Registered Medical Practitioner.
Allergy Alert: Strictly contraindicated in individuals with a known history of severe allergic reactions to penicillin, cephalosporins, or beta-lactam drugs.
Medical History: Use with extreme caution in patients with underlying kidney impairment, severe liver disorders, or history of jaundice.
Storage & Handling
Store in a cool, dry & dark place below 25°C.
Keep the medicine out of reach of children.
