Product Overview
Reate-AM is a prescription-only combination tablet formulated to manage hypertension (high blood pressure). It combines an angiotensin II receptor blocker (ARB) with a calcium channel blocker (CCB) to provide dual-action control, lowering blood pressure effectively and reducing the long-term risk of stroke, heart attack, or kidney complications.
Key Benefits & Uses
Hypertension Management: Lowers elevated blood pressure when a single active agent is not sufficient.
Cardiovascular Protection: Reduces overall workload on the heart, minimizing future risk of heart attacks and strokes.
Dual Mechanism Action: Relaxes and widens targeted blood vessels to ensure smoother, unrestricted blood flow throughout the body.
Composition & Active Ingredients
Each film-coated tablet contains:
Olmesartan Medoxomil IP: 20 mg (Angiotensin II Receptor Blocker) — Relaxes blood vessels by blocking the action of a natural substance that causes them to tighten.
Amlodipine Besylate IP: Equivalent to Amlodipine 5 mg (Calcium Channel Blocker) — Inhibits calcium entry into vascular smooth muscle, keeping blood vessels dilated.
Excipients: q.s. (Quantity Sufficient)
Colour: Titanium Dioxide IP
Safety Warnings & Precautions
Pregnancy Warning: Strictly contraindicated during pregnancy, as it can cause serious harm or risk to an unborn baby.
Schedule H Drug: Prescription required. This medication must not be sold by retail without a valid medical practitioner's prescription.
Side Effect Alert: May cause mild side effects such as ankle swelling (edema), flushing, dizziness, headaches, or tiredness.
Regular Monitoring: Patients should regularly track their blood pressure and kidney function parameters while undergoing active therapy.
Storage & Handling
Store in a cool, dry, and dark place.
Protect carefully from direct light exposure and moisture.
Keep securely out of reach and sight of children.
