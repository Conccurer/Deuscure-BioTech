Product Overview
RUXCEF-OF is a prescription-only combination antibiotic tablet formulated to treat a wide variety of bacterial infections. It combines a cephalosporin antibiotic with a fluoroquinolone antibiotic, providing a broad-spectrum dual action that destroys susceptible bacteria and prevents them from reproducing.
Key Benefits & Uses
Typhoid Fever: Effectively treats enteric fever and severe gastrointestinal bacterial infections.
Respiratory Tract Infections: Manages bacterial bronchitis, pneumonia, and severe sinusitis.
Urinary Tract Infections (UTIs): Resolves complicated and uncomplicated urinary tract and bladder infections.
Reproductive Tract Infections: Treats specific pelvic inflammatory diseases and sexually transmitted bacterial infections.
Composition & Active Ingredients
Each film-coated tablet contains:
Cefixime IP (as Trihydrate) equivalent to anhydrous Cefixime: 200 mg (Cephalosporin Antibiotic) — Inhibits bacterial cell wall synthesis, causing the bacteria to rupture and die.
Ofloxacin IP: 200 mg (Fluoroquinolone Antibiotic) — Prevents bacterial DNA replication, stopping the multiplication and spread of the infection.
Excipients: q.s. (Quantity Sufficient)
Colour: Titanium Dioxide IP
Safety Warnings & Precautions
Complete the Course: Patients must finish the full prescribed course to prevent the development of drug-resistant bacteria.
Schedule H1 Drug: Warning: It is dangerous to take this preparation except in accordance with medical advice. Not to be sold by retail without the prescription of a Registered Medical Practitioner.
Side Effect Alert: Common mild side effects include diarrhea, nausea, abdominal pain, dizziness, or temporary loss of appetite.
Medical History: Use with extreme caution in patients with a history of tendonitis, neurological disorders, or kidney impairment.
Storage & Handling
Store in a cool and dry place below 25°C.
Protected from direct sunlight and moisture.
Keep the medicines out of reach of children.
