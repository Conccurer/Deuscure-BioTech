Product Overview
Mie Plus Tonic is a comprehensive, Ayurvedic uterine tonic formulated specifically to support female reproductive health and physical vitality. Combining proven traditional botanical extracts, this herbal remedy helps manage irregular menstrual cycles, controls excessive white vaginal discharge, and combats general bodily weakness or fatigue in women.
Key Benefits & Uses
Regulates Menstrual Cycles: Formulated to help correct hormonal imbalances, manage period difficulties, and ease menstrual cramps.
Manages Abnormal Discharge: Specifically helps control white vaginal discharge (leucorrhea) and maintain localized tissue health.
Combats Female Body Weakness: Fortified with rejuvenating herbs that restore energy levels, enhance physical strength, and reduce the fatigue associated with reproductive disorders.
Uterine Health Support: Strengthens and tones the uterine muscles to support long-term gynecological well-being.
Composition & Herbal Ingredients
Each 10 ml contains an extensive blend of Ayurvedic herbs:
Ashok Chhal (Saraca asoca): 1800 mg — Traditional uterine health rejuvenator.
Punarnava (Boerhavia diffusa): 200 mg — Supports detoxification and reduces systemic swelling.
Arjun Chhal (Terminalia arjuna): 200 mg — High-potency antioxidant tonic.
Babul Chhal (Acacia arabica): 200 mg — Known for its natural astringent and healing properties.
Gokhru (Tribulus terrestris): 150 mg — Aids hormonal balance and urinary function.
Majufal (Quercus infectoria): 150 mg — Helps control abnormal, excessive fluid discharges.
Ashwagandha (Withania somnifera): 150 mg — Powerful adaptogen that eliminates physical fatigue and mental stress.
Shatavar (Asparagus racemosus): 150 mg — Classic female reproductive tonic supporting vitality.
Jeera (Cuminum cyminum): 150 mg — Aids healthy digestive function and metabolic rate.
Lodhar (Symplocos racemosa): 150 mg — Widely used to manage gynecological and menstrual complaints.
Additional Botanicals: Nag Kesar (100 mg), Darhu Haldi (100 mg), Kachur (100 mg), Bala (100 mg), Anar Chhal (100 mg).
Base: Sugar base q.s. (Quantity Sufficient) with added sodium benzoate as a preservative, color, and flavor.
Dosage Instructions
Adults: 2 teaspoonfuls taken 2–3 times a day.
Children: 1 teaspoonful taken 2–3 times a day.
Or as explicitly directed by the Physician.
Safety Warnings & Precautions
Ayurvedic Proprietary Medicine: Formulated under Indian traditional systems; it is not meant to replace specialized medical care for complex gynecological emergencies.
Pregnancy Caution: Expectant mothers must consult a qualified doctor or gynecologist before starting any uterine formulation.
Sugar Advisory: Contains a sugar syrup base; individuals monitoring blood glucose profiles should manage usage boundaries carefully.
Storage & Handling
Store safely in a cool, dry, and dark place.
Shake well before use to ensure even distribution of the herbal blend.
Keep securely out of reach of children.
