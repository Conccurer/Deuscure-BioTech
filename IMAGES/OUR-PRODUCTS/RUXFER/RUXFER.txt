Product Overview
RUXFER is a premium, FSSAI-approved nutraceutical supplement systematically formulated to support healthy red blood cell production, improve hemoglobin levels, and combat nutritional iron deficiencies. By blending a gentle, highly bioavailable form of iron with vital blood-building vitamins and antioxidant botanical extracts, this daily tablet helps restore physical energy and general vitality.
Key Benefits & Uses
Comprehensive Anemia Management: Replenishes depleted bodily iron reserves to optimize active hemoglobin levels and oxygen transportation.
Boosted Energy Levels: Directly counteracts the constant physical exhaustion, dizziness, muscle weakness, and fatigue related to nutritional deficiencies.
Enhanced Iron Absorption: Formulated with specialized co-factors that maximize iron uptake in the gut while remaining gentle on the stomach lining.
Immune & Cellular Support: Combines essential immune-boosting antioxidants to reinforce natural defense mechanisms and neutralize cellular stress.
Composition & Nutritional Information
Each film-coated tablet contains (Approx.):
Ferric di-phosphate (Ferric Pyrophosphate) equivalent to Elemental Iron: 30 mg — Provides a highly stable, gastrointestinal-friendly source of iron to construct red blood cells.
Vitamin C: 50 mg (62.5% RDA) — Promotes advanced intestinal absorption of iron by converting it into an easily absorbable form.

Vitamin B12: 0.75 mcg (34.09% RDA) — Supports optimal neurological function and works alongside folate to prevent megaloblastic anemia.
Curcuma Longa: 5 mg — A natural herbal extract containing active curcuminoids to manage underlying inflammation.
