Product Overview
DUESLIN-16 is a prescription-only medication formulated to treat balance disorders and inner ear conditions. It contains Betahistine, an analogue of histamine that improves microcirculation inside the inner ear, helping to alleviate debilitating episodes of spinning sensations, dizziness, and related auditory symptoms.
Key Benefits & Uses
Meniere's Disease Management: Effectively reduces the frequency and severity of vertigo (spinning sensations) episodes, tinnitus (ringing in the ears), and progressive hearing loss associated with Meniere’s syndrome.
Vertigo Symptom Relief: Alleviates general vestibular vertigo and inner-ear-related dizziness to improve everyday physical balance.
Enhances Blood Flow: Stimulates localized blood circulation within the inner ear to decrease fluid pressure buildup.
Composition & Active Ingredients
Each uncoated tablet contains:
Betahistine Dihydrochloride IP: 16 mg (Histaminergic Vestibular Agent)
Excipients: q.s. (Quantity Sufficient)
Safety Warnings & Precautions
Schedule H Drug: Prescription required. This medication should not be sold by retail without a valid medical prescription from a Registered Medical Practitioner.
Gastrointestinal Advice: Can cause mild gastric discomfort. It is highly recommended to take this medication with or after meals to minimize stomach irritation.
Medical History Contraindications: Use with extreme caution or avoid in individuals with active peptic ulcers or asthma (pheochromocytoma histories), as histamine-like agents can worsen these conditions.
Pregnancy and Lactation: Consult a healthcare professional before use, as safety parameters during pregnancy have not been fully established.
Storage & Handling
Store in a cool & dry place below 25°C temperature.
Protect carefully from direct light & moisture.
Keep the medicine out of reach of children.
