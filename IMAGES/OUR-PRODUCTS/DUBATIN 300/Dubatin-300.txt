Product Overview
Dubatin-300 is a prescription-only neurological medication formulated to treat neuropathic pain (nerve pain) and manage specific seizure disorders. It contains Gabapentin, an anticonvulsant and analgesic agent that alters the way the body senses pain signals, providing effective relief from chronic, burning, or shooting nerve discomfort.
Key Benefits & Uses
Neuropathic Pain Management: Relieves chronic nerve pain caused by conditions such as shingles (postherpetic neuralgia), spinal cord injuries, or peripheral neuropathy.
Epilepsy & Seizure Control: Used alongside other medications to treat and control partial seizures in adults and children.
Stabilizes Nerve Activity: Calms overactive or damaged nerves in the body to decrease pain transmission to the brain.
Composition & Active Ingredients
Each film-coated tablet contains:
Gabapentin IP: 300 mg (Neurological Analgesic / Anticonvulsant)
Excipients: q.s. (Quantity Sufficient)
Colour: Red Oxide of Iron
Safety Warnings & Precautions
Schedule H Drug: Prescription required. This medication should not be sold by retail without a valid medical practitioner's prescription.
Drowsiness Alert: Can cause significant sleepiness, dizziness, or blurred vision. Advise patients not to drive, operate machinery, or perform tasks requiring sharp mental alertness after use.
Avoid Sudden Discontinuation: Advise users never to stop taking this medication abruptly, as sudden withdrawal can cause a return of seizures or severe anxiety symptoms.
Alcohol Interaction: Avoid alcohol consumption while taking this medication as it can drastically worsen dizziness and drowsiness.
Storage & Handling
Store in a cool and dry place below 25°C temperature.
Protect from direct light and moisture.
Keep the medicine out of reach of children.
