Product Overview
Ruxmab Tablets are a prescription-only triple-action combination medicine formulated to provide relief from severe pain, swelling, and inflammation. By combining a potent non-steroidal anti-inflammatory drug (NSAID), an analgesic-antipyretic, and a proteolytic enzyme, this medication is widely used to treat acute musculoskeletal disorders, joint pain, and post-operative inflammation.
Key Benefits & Uses
Comprehensive Pain Relief: Effectively targets moderate to severe pain associated with osteoarthritis, rheumatoid arthritis, ankylosing spondylitis, and muscle spasms.
Reduces Swelling and Edema: The enzymatic action helps break down abnormal proteins at the site of injury, accelerating fluid drainage and reducing localized tissue swelling.
Post-Injury and Post-Surgery Recovery: Promotes faster healing by simultaneously relieving pain, reducing inflammation, and easing tissue repair after minor surgeries or trauma.
Composition & Active Ingredients
Each film-coated tablet contains:
Diclofenac Potassium BP: 50 mg (Non-Steroidal Anti-Inflammatory Drug) — Blocks cyclooxygenase (COX) enzymes to quickly reduce the production of chemical messengers that cause pain and swelling.
Paracetamol IP: 325 mg (Analgesic & Antipyretic) — Acts on the central nervous system to elevate the pain threshold and reduce fever.
Serratiopeptidase IP: 10 mg (equivalent to enteric-coated 20000 Serratiopeptidase units) — A proteolytic enzyme that thins inflammatory fluids and enhances tissue penetration of the pain-relieving components.
Excipients: q.s. (Quantity Sufficient)
Colour: Red oxide of Iron
Safety Warnings & Precautions
Schedule H Drug: Prescription required. This medication should not be sold by retail without a valid medical practitioner's prescription.
Liver Warning: Contains paracetamol. Taking more than the daily dose may cause serious liver damage or severe allergic reactions (e.g., swelling of the face, mouth, and throat, difficulty in breathing, itching, or rash).
Gastrointestinal Alert: Prolonged use of diclofenac may cause stomach irritation, peptic ulcers, or acidity. It is recommended to take this medication with food or an antacid.
Contraindications: Use with caution or avoid in individuals with preexisting blood clotting disorders, severe kidney disease, or a history of allergic reactions to aspirin or other NSAIDs.
Storage & Handling
Store in a cool, dry, and dark place.
Protect carefully from direct light exposure and moisture.
Keep the medicine out of reach of children.
