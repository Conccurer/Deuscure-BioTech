Product Overview
Duxip Suspension is a combination oral medication categorized under gastroprotective agents and local anesthetics. It features a potent blend of Sucralfate and Oxetacaine, formulated specifically to alleviate the burning discomfort of severe acidity, gastritis, and peptic ulcers. By forming a physical defensive coating over the gastric lining, it insulates raw tissue from acidic breakdown while offering rapid, localized numbing relief.

Key Benefits & Uses
- Fast-Acting Relief: Features Oxetacaine, a strong local anesthetic that rapidly numbs the stomach lining to halt burning pain and severe hyperacidity.
- Ulcer Protective Barrier: Contains Sucralfate, which directly binds to exudates on damaged ulcer sites to form a mechanical shield against stomach acid, pepsin, and bile.
- Mucosal Healing: Safely insulates the raw stomach lining, supporting natural tissue repair from inflammation caused by gastritis or chemical irritation.

Composition & Nutritional Information
Please make sure to double-check the physical label on your specific product stock to confirm exact strength parameters. Each 10 ml of the oral suspension typically contains:
- Sucralfate USP: 1000 mg (1g) — Operates as a localized gastroprotective and anti-ulcer shield.
- Oxetacaine BP (also known as Oxethazaine): 20 mg — Operates as a fast-acting, surface-active topical anesthetic stable in acidic environments.
- Base Details: Flavored syrupy sugar-free matrix (Mint Flavour).

Directions for Use
- Shake well before use: Always vigorously shake the bottle to distribute the suspended active elements evenly before pouring.
- Optimal Timing: Take this medication strictly on an empty stomach, ideally 1 hour before scheduled meals or 2 hours afterward.
- Avoid immediate liquids: Instruct users to avoid drinking water or beverages immediately after a dose to preserve the protective coat across the gastric wall.
- Avoid overlapping antacids: Do not ingest standard antacid formulations within 30 minutes before or after consuming this product.

Safety Warnings & Precautions
- Medical Class Designation: This is a Schedule H Prescription Drug and must be taken under strict medical supervision.
- Kidney Impairment Caution: Use with extreme care in individuals suffering from chronic kidney conditions or undergoing dialysis due to risks of structural aluminum buildup.
- Drug Cross-Interactions: This product can bind to and decrease the systemic absorption of other prescription drugs, such as tetracyclines, fluoroquinolones, and thyroid supplements. Spaced dosing windows are mandatory.
- Potential Side Effects: May trigger temporary constipation. Recommend a higher dietary fiber intake and adequate hydration to manage regular digestion.

Storage & Handling
- Temperature Thresholds: Keep the bottle stored safely in a dry space below 25°C. Protect it from freezing cycles.
- Light Defense: Store the formulation inside its original outer protective carton to isolate it from direct sun rays and excessive heat exposure.
- Child Safety Measures: Securely store the medication out of reach of children and family pets.
