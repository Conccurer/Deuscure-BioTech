Product Overview
Deuvit Powder is a sugar-free, chocolate-flavored protein food supplement fortified with antioxidants, multivitamins, and multiminerals. It is designed as a balanced nutritional drink to support daily strength, enhance immunity, and improve overall vitality for individuals needing an extra boost in their everyday nutrition.

Key Benefits & Uses
- Strength and Muscle Maintenance: Supplies essential dietary protein to support lean muscle mass and structural strength.
- Immunity Support: Enriched with multivitamins and cellular antioxidants to help reinforce the body’s natural immune responses.
- Energy and Vitality Boost: Formulated with an array of multivitamins and multiminerals to convert food into cellular energy efficiently, combating fatigue and daily weakness.

- Multivitamins & Multiminerals: A comprehensive blend of everyday micronutrients optimized for comprehensive metabolic health.
- Sugar-Free Base & Chocolate Flavour: Formulated to be diet-friendly and highly palatable without adding processed sugars.

Directions for Use
- Prepare the drink: Take a glass of lukewarm or cold milk (or water, as advised by your healthcare provider).
- Mix thoroughly: Add the recommended number of scoops (typically 1–2 tablespoons or as prescribed) of Deuvit powder. Stir rapidly until completely dissolved to avoid clumping.
- Consumption frequency: Drink it immediately after mixing, preferably once or twice daily alongside a meal or post-activity.

Safety Warnings & Precautions
- Allergen Notice: Check the detailed breakdown on the back panel if you have known structural sensitivities to soy, milk dairy, or specific protein concentrates.
- Medical Supervision: Consult a doctor prior to routine consumption if you are pregnant, nursing, or managing chronic conditions like kidney disorders.
- Not a Meal Replacement: Use this product as a dietary supplement alongside a well-balanced, wholesome daily diet rather than a complete food replacement.

Storage & Handling
- Keep Sealed: Ensure the plastic lid is tightly screwed shut immediately after every single use to block ambient moisture and humidity.
- Environment: Keep stored in a cool, dry room away from intense heat sources or direct sunlight exposure.
- Child Safety: Store the container safely in an elevated spot out of the standard reach of young children.
