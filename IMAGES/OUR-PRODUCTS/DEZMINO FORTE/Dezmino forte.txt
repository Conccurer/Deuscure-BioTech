Product Overview
Dezmino Forte is a specialized nutritional and neuroprotective supplement formulated with key B-vitamins and coenzymes . It is primarily prescribed to support central and peripheral nerve function, encourage optimal red blood cell production, and bridge nutritional gaps .

Key Benefits & Uses
- Nerve Regeneration: Helps repair damaged nerve fibers and relieves symptoms of neuropathic pain or tingling sensations .
- Red Blood Cell Production: Prevents macrocytic anemia by assisting in proper DNA synthesis and red blood cell maturation .
- Energy Metabolism: Boosts systemic cellular energy by efficiently converting carbohydrates, fats, and proteins into usable fuel .

Composition & Nutritional Information
Please make sure to double-check the physical label on your box to confirm specific milligram distributions, as manufacturer batches can vary . The active ingredients printed on the box include :
- Methylcobalamin: An active, highly bioavailable coenzyme form of Vitamin B12 essential for the protective myelin sheath surrounding nerves .
- Adenosylcobalamin: Another active form of Vitamin B12 that plays a critical role in cellular energy production within mitochondria .
- Vitamin B1 (Thiamine): Crucial for glucose metabolism and supporting fundamental nerve signaling .
- Vitamin B2 (Riboflavin): Supports cellular respiration and the structural activation of other vitamins .
- Vitamin B6 (Pyridoxine): Plays an essential role in neurotransmitter synthesis and the metabolism of red blood cells .
- Folic Acid (Vitamin B9): Vital for overall cell division, amino acid metabolism, and healthy nervous system function .

Directions for Use
- Follow prescription: Take this medicine in the exact dose and duration prescribed by your healthcare professional .
- Swallow whole: Take the capsule intact with a glass of water; do not crush, open, or chew it .
- Take with meals: It is generally recommended to take it alongside or immediately after a meal to optimize the absorption of B-vitamins and minimize any potential stomach irritation .

Safety Warnings & Precautions
- Pregnancy & Breastfeeding: Inform your doctor before starting this medication if you are pregnant, planning a pregnancy, or breastfeeding .
- Allergies & Sensitivities: Do not take this product if you have a known history of hypersensitivity to Methylcobalamin or any other B-complex components .
- Pre-existing Conditions: Discuss with your doctor if you have a history of liver conditions, kidney disorders, or low potassium levels (hypokalemia) .

Storage & Handling
- Temperature: Keep stored in a cool, dry place safely below 25°C to 30°C .
- Protection: Keep the blister strips inside the original outer cardboard box to shield the capsules from direct heat, moisture, and sunlight .
- Safety: Ensure the package is stored completely out of sight and reach of children and household pets .
