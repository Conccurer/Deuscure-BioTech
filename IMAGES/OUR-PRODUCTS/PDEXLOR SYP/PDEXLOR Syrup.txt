Product Overview
PDEXLOR Syrup (100ml) is a sugar-free, cherry-flavored combination oral liquid designed for the effective relief of dry cough, nasal congestion, and common cold or respiratory allergy symptoms. It combines a proven cough suppressant, a decongestant, and an antihistamine to provide multi-symptom relief.
Key Benefits & Uses
Suppresses Dry Cough: Directly targets the cough reflex center to reduce non-productive, irritating coughs.
Clears Nasal Congestion: Relieves stuffy nose and sinus pressure, making breathing easier.
Alleviates Allergy Symptoms: Effectively controls runny nose, sneezing, itchy throat, and watery eyes.
Diabetic-Friendly Formula: Sugar-free syrup base suitable for patients monitoring carbohydrate intake.
Composition & Active Ingredients
Each 5 ml of PDEXLOR Syrup contains:
Dextromethorphan Hydrobromide IP: 10 mg (Antitussive / Cough Suppressant)
Phenylephrine Hydrochloride IP: 5 mg (Nasal Decongestant)
Chlorpheniramine Maleate IP: 2 mg (Antihistamine)
Base: Pleasantly flavored sugar-free syrup base (Cherry Flavor)
Safety Warnings & Precautions
Age Restriction: Contraindicated for use in children below 4 years of age.
Drowsiness Alert: May cause sleepiness. Advise users not to drive or operate heavy machinery after consumption.
Alcohol Interaction: Avoid alcohol consumption while taking this medication as it can worsen drowsiness.
Medical Conditions: Consult a doctor before use if the patient has high blood pressure, heart disease, thyroid disorders, diabetes, or glaucoma.
Storage & Handling
Store at a temperature not exceeding 30°C.
Protect from direct light and moisture.
Keep the bottle tightly closed after each use.
Keep out of reach of children.
