Product Overview
RUXTOL D3 is a high-potency Vitamin D3 supplement formulated in easy-to-swallow softgel capsules. It is primarily used to treat and prevent Vitamin D deficiency, support bone mineralization, and manage bone disorders such as osteoporosis, osteomalacia, and rickets. Each capsule delivers a concentrated dose of Cholecalciferol to effectively restore depleted Vitamin D reserves in the body.
Key Benefits & Uses
Treats Vitamin D Deficiency: Rapidly replenishes low systemic Vitamin D levels to combat associated fatigue and muscle weakness.
Enhances Calcium Absorption: Optimizes the absorption of calcium and phosphorus from food, which is vital for building and maintaining strong bones and teeth.
Bone Health Support: Helps prevent progressive bone density loss, reducing the susceptibility to fractures in older adults and osteoporosis patients.
Immune System Boost: Plays an essential regulatory role in immune cell function to enhance the body's natural defense mechanisms.
Composition & Active Ingredients
Each soft gelatin capsule contains:
Cholecalciferol USP (Vitamin D3): 60000 IU — The active form of Vitamin D naturally synthesized by the skin upon sun exposure, essential for calcium homeostasis.
Excipients: q.s. (Quantity Sufficient)
Note: Appropriate overages of vitamins are added to compensate for any natural loss during storage.
Safety Warnings & Precautions
Administration Guideline: Must be swallowed whole with water. Do not chew, open, or crush the softgel capsules.
Dosage Caution: This is a high-dose therapeutic supplement usually taken once a week or as explicitly directed by a physician. Avoid unsupervised daily intake to prevent vitamin toxicity (hypercalcemia).
Prescription Caution: This preparation should be used in accordance with expert medical advice and not be sold by retail without a valid prescription.
Medical Consultation: Patients with kidney stones, severe renal impairment, or high baseline calcium levels must consult a doctor before starting therapy.
Storage & Handling
Store in a cool, dry, and dark place below 25°C.
Protect thoroughly from direct sunlight, heat, and moisture.
Specialized Care: Softgel capsules are prone to leaking, melting, or changing specifications if stored in environments exceeding 25°C.
Keep securely out of reach and sight of children.
