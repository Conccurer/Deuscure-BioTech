Product Overview
DEZMINO PLUS Tablets (subtitled "The Rejuvenating Exhilarate") is an advanced, high-potency nutritional dietary food supplement formulated to optimize cardiovascular health, nerve function, and cellular energy production. By pairing Ubiquinol—the highly absorbable form of Coenzyme Q10—with fatty acid transporters, essential omega-3s, and vital nerve cofactors, it provides a comprehensive approach to combating metabolic fatigue, supporting heart health, and promoting healthy cellular aging.
Key Benefits & Uses
Advanced Cellular Energy: Ubiquinol and L-Carnitine work together within the mitochondria to convert dietary fats into cellular energy (ATP), reducing systemic muscle fatigue.
Cardiovascular Support: Provides Omega-3 Fatty Acids to help maintain healthy lipid profiles, lower oxidative stress, and support general heart function.
Neurological & Nerve Care: Delivers Cyanocobalamin (Vitamin B12) to help build the protective myelin sheath around nerves, addressing symptoms of nerve exhaustion.
Enhanced Antioxidant Protection: Ubiquinol offers powerful, direct antioxidant protection to safeguard cell membranes from premature oxidative damage.
