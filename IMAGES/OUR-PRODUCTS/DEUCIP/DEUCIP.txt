Product Overview
DEUCIP Electrolyte is a refreshing and energizing oral rehydration powder formulated to support quick recovery from dehydration. Classified as a proprietary dietary food nutritional supplement, it features a tangy orange flavor enriched with Vitamin C, glucose, and essential electrolytes to instantly restore energy and maintain fluid balance.
Key Benefits & Uses
Quick Dehydration Recovery: Restores critical water and electrolyte balances lost due to heavy sweating, heat exhaustion, or physical exertion.
Instant Energy Source: Formulated with glucose and fast-digesting carbohydrates to provide an immediate energy surge.
Immune Support: Enriched with Vitamin C to help fight off oxidative stress and bolster general wellness.
Refreshing Orange Flavor: Offers a tangy, tasty drink alternative to standard hydration solutions.

Directions for Use
Add four teaspoons full (approx. 20 gms) of DEUCIP powder to a glass (approx. 200 ml) of cool water.
Stir thoroughly until completely dissolved.
Use only after reconstitution and in the recommended dilution.
Safety Warnings & Precautions
Category Classification: This product is a Proprietary Dietary Food Nutritional Supplement. It is strictly not for medicinal use.
Sugar Content Notice: Contains a substantial amount of sucrose and carbohydrates; individuals managing diabetes should use caution or consult a healthcare professional before use.
Health Specialist Consultation: If you are experiencing severe, prolonged vomiting, diarrhea, or acute dehydration, please consult a medical expert immediately.
Storage & Handling
Store in a cool and dry place.
Protect from light and keep away from moisture.
Close the sachet or container securely after each use to prevent powder clumping.
