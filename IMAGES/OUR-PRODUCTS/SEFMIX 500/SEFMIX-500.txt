Product Overview
SEFMIX-500 is a premium prescription-only antibiotic tablet formulated to treat a wide variety of bacterial infections. It contains Cefuroxime Axetil, a powerful second-generation cephalosporin antibiotic that works effectively by stopping the growth and multiplication of susceptible bacteria in the body.
Key Benefits & Uses
Respiratory Tract Infections: Treats bacterial infections of the lungs and throat, including bronchitis, pneumonia, tonsillitis, and pharyngitis.
Ear, Nose, and Throat Infections: Effectively resolves acute bacterial sinusitis and otitis media (middle ear infections).
Urinary Tract Infections (UTIs): Manages complicated and uncomplicated bacterial bladder and urinary tract infections.
Skin and Soft Tissue Infections: Resolves bacterial infections affecting the skin layers and underlying soft tissues.
Lyme Disease: Used in early stages to treat Lyme disease caused by specific tick-borne bacteria.
Composition & Active Ingredients
Each film-coated tablet contains:
Cefuroxime Axetil IP equivalent to Cefuroxime: 500 mg — Destroys bacteria by targeting and inhibiting their cell wall synthesis, causing the bacterial cells to rupture and die.
Excipients: q.s. (Quantity Sufficient)
Colour: Titanium Dioxide I.P.
Safety Warnings & Precautions
Complete the Full Course: Patients must finish the entire prescribed course of this antibiotic, even if symptoms clear up early, to prevent the development of drug-resistant bacteria.
Schedule H Drug: Warning: Not to be sold by retail without the prescription of a Registered Medical Practitioner.
Side Effect Alert: Common mild side effects include nausea, diarrhea, dizziness, stomach pain, or a temporary rise in liver enzymes.
Allergy Warning: Use with caution if the patient has a history of allergies to penicillin or other cephalosporin antibiotics, as cross-reactivity can occur.
Storage & Handling
Store in a cool, dry, and dark place below 25°C.
Protect carefully from direct sunlight and moisture.
Keep the medicine out of reach and sight of children.
