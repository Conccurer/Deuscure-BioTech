Product Overview
RUXTOL Injection is a ultra-high potency, prescription-only Vitamin D3 treatment delivered in a sterile, oily base solution. It is specifically designed to manage severe, clinical Vitamin D deficiencies, malabsorption syndromes, and metabolic bone diseases where oral supplementation is ineffective or impractical. Each ampoule delivers a concentrated dose of Cholecalciferol directly via muscular administration to quickly replenish vital bodily reserves.
Key Benefits & Uses
Correction of Severe Deficiency: Swiftly treats profound Vitamin D deficiency in patients unable to absorb oral supplements due to gastrointestinal issues.
Bone Mineralization Support: Enhances systemic calcium and phosphorus utilization to combat severe osteomalacia, rickets, and acute bone loss.
Management of Osteoporosis: Used as an adjunctive therapeutic intervention to reinforce bone density and lower the risk of fragility fractures in high-risk patients.
Composition & Active Ingredients
Each ml contains:
Cholecalciferol IP: 600000 IU (15mg) — An ultra-concentrated dose of active Vitamin D3 to rapidly correct severe physiological deficiency states.
Sterile Oily Base: q.s. (Quantity Sufficient) — Engineered for sustained, gradual drug release from the tissue depot following an intramuscular injection.
Safety Warnings & Precautions
Route of Administration: Strictly for Intramuscular (I.M.) use only. Must never be administered intravenously.
Professional Administration: Should only be handled and administered by a registered nurse, physician, or qualified healthcare professional.
Quality Visual Check: Warning: If the solution is not clear or has suspended particles, please do not use and return for replacement.
Toxicity Warning: Because this is an exceptionally high-potency formula, regular clinical tracking of serum calcium, phosphorus, and kidney function is essential to avoid hypercalcemia.
Prescription Caution: This preparation should be used strictly under clinical supervision and must not be sold by retail without a valid medical prescription.
Storage & Handling
Store safely at a temperature below 25°C.
Protect carefully from direct sunlight, light exposure, and moisture.
Do not freeze.
Keep securely out of reach and sight of children.
