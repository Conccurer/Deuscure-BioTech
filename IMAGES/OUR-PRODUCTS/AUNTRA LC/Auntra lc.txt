Product Overview
Auntra-LC Tablets is a specialized combination health supplement designed to support cellular energy production, nerve health, and muscle recovery. By combining essential amino acid derivatives, potent antioxidants, and highly bioavailable minerals, this formulation helps manage muscle cramps, physical fatigue, and metabolic stress while promoting overall muscular and neurological vitality.

Key Benefits & Uses
- Reduces Muscle Cramps & Fatigue: Helps relieve nocturnal leg cramps, muscle spasms, and soreness by supporting electrolyte balance and cellular relaxation.
- Boosts Cellular Energy: L-Carnitine L-Tartrate facilitates the transport of fatty acids into cellular mitochondria, turning fat into usable energy for daily physical activity.
- Antioxidant Defense: Vitamin E acts as a fat-soluble antioxidant, protecting cell membranes and muscular tissue from oxidative damage.
- Supports Muscle & Nerve Health: Magnesium Bisglycinate is a highly absorbable form of magnesium that supports smooth muscle contraction, nerve signal transmission, and cardiovascular wellness.

Directions for Use
- Usage: Take the tablet strictly as directed by your healthcare professional or physician.
- Administration: Swallow the tablet whole with a glass of water. Do not crush, chew, or break it.
- Timing: Recommended to be taken after meals to minimize any potential gastrointestinal discomfort and maximize nutrient absorption.

Safety Warnings & Precautions
- Medical Class Designation: Schedule H Prescription Drug Caution — Not to be sold by retail without the prescription of a Registered Medical Practitioner.
- Child Safety: Keep the medicine strictly out of reach and sight of children.
- Pregnancy & Nursing: Consult your doctor prior to use if you are pregnant, planning to become pregnant, or breastfeeding.
- Health Conditions: Individuals with pre-existing kidney disorders or known hypermagnesemia should seek professional medical advice before usage.

Storage & Handling
- Temperature Threshold: Store in a cool, dry, and dark place at a temperature not exceeding 30°C.
- Light Defense: Keep protected from direct sunlight, moisture, and excess humidity.
